✂️ Auto-Cut
Auto-Cut skips sponsors/subscribe reminders and speeds through the dull parts. Toggle it per video with the ✂️ button, or leave "Enable Auto-Cut" on to apply automatically. SponsorBlock sends only a privacy-preserving hash of the video ID. AI highlights use your own Anthropic key and send the transcript to Claude. Changes apply on the next video / reload. Tip: press Shift+C on a video to toggle Auto-Cut.
